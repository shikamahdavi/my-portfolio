The Hiring Thesis

Why this role. Why Shika. Why now.

Fidelity Brokerage is moving toward a product-oriented learning model. That requires someone who can bring structure to ambiguity, build product discipline, lead learning designers, and connect learning to business outcomes. Dr. Mahdavi has already done this work in a regulated financial services environment.

Why This Role

Fidelity Brokerage is shifting from a training function to a product-oriented learning model. That shift requires someone who thinks in portfolios, roadmaps, and outcomes, not just courses and completions. The Director, Learning Products role is a product leadership role wearing a learning title.

Why Shika

Dr. Mahdavi has already run learning as a product function inside a regulated financial services environment. She has built the operating model, not just the content. She has led designers, negotiated with executives, defined governance and decision rights, and measured results in the language business leaders use: readiness, adoption, performance, and revenue impact.

Why Now

Fidelity Brokerage is early in this transition. The first leader in this seat will define how the function operates for years. That work rewards someone who has done it before, at a comparable scale, with the same regulatory weight and executive scrutiny. Waiting costs Fidelity a year of trial and error that has already been solved elsewhere.

This is not a resume. This is the business case for the hire.
